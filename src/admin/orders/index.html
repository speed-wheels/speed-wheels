    How an order works

    1. A customer pays. The order appears here under To pack. You get a sound and pop-up if alerts are on and this page is open. Razorpay also records every payment in your Razorpay dashboard and app.
    2. Print the packing slip. Tick the orders, press Packing slips, and print (or save as PDF). Pick the items using the slip and put it in the box.
    3. Pack it. Press Mark packed. The customer's tracking page now shows "Packed".
    4. Ship it. Print a Shipping label (4×6 inch, or on A4 and cut it out) or use the courier's own label. Book the parcel with your courier (India Post, Delhivery, DTDC, Shiprocket…).
    5. Add the tracking number. Open the order, set status to Shipped, choose the courier and type the AWB / tracking number, then Save. The customer sees it on speedwheels.in/track and gets an email (once email is set up).
    6. Delivered. When the courier shows delivered, mark it Delivered (one by one or in bulk).
    7. Cancel or refund. Refund the money in your Razorpay dashboard (Payments → the payment → Refund), then set the order here to Refunded or Cancelled.

    Good to know

    • The "From" address on labels and slips comes from Products & settings → Settings → Shop details. Add your shipping-from address and phone there once.
    • Unpaid lists people who opened payment but didn't pay. You don't need to do anything with them.
    • Excel (CSV) downloads the orders you are looking at, for your accounts.
    • This page works on your phone too. Add it to your home screen for quick access.
