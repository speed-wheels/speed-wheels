**Cancellations.** You can cancel an order within 12 hours of placing it, or any time before it is dispatched, for a full refund. Custom orders cannot be cancelled once printing has started.

**Returns.** If your stand arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos or an unboxing video. We will replace it or give you a full refund, including shipping. Return shipping for these cases is free.

**Change of mind.** Because each stand is printed to order, we do not accept returns for change of mind, except where the product is unused and in its original packaging and you contact us within 7 days of delivery. In that case, return shipping is paid by you and we will refund the product price after we receive it.

**Not returnable.** Custom-designed or personalised stands, unless they arrive damaged or defective.

**Refunds.** Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account). Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

**How to request.** Message us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/{{ site.whatsapp }}) or email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) with your order details.
