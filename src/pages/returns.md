title: Returns, refunds and cancellations
description: Returns, refunds and cancellation policy for Speed Wheels display stands.
updated: 24 November 2026

**Cancellations.** You can cancel an order within 12 hours of placing it, or any time before it is dispatched, for a full refund. Custom orders cannot be cancelled once printing has started.

**Returns.** If your item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos or an unboxing video. We will replace it or give you a full refund, including shipping. Return shipping for these cases is free.

**Change of mind.** You can return unused, unopened items in their original packaging within 7 days of delivery. Return shipping is paid by you, and we refund the product price after we receive and check the item. Custom or personalised stands cannot be returned for change of mind.

**Not returnable.** Custom-designed or personalised stands, unless they arrive damaged or defective.

**Refunds.** Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account). Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
